Zerker is **open-core**. Everything needed to self-host, run, and secure the
gateway — the catalog, routing and proxy, MCP-native transport, auth and
per-tenant credential isolation, and the x402 payment **gate** — is OSS and
free. The <Commercial /> managed tier picks up where the open-core line is
drawn: at **moving money** and at **running Zerker across many tenants from
one place**. See [The open-core boundary](/concepts/open-core-boundary/)
for the reasoning, and [OSS vs Commercial at a glance](/oss-vs-commercial/)
for the capability-by-capability split.

## Settlement & collection

The OSS gateway **verifies** an x402 payment authorization before forwarding a
call. Actually **collecting** that payment on-chain — settling the authorization
and moving the funds — is the managed tier, including a **hosted facilitator**
so you don't have to run and fund the `/settle` server yourself. (You can also
self-host a facilitator entirely on OSS — see
[Self-hosting vs managed](/facilitator/self-hosting/); the managed
facilitator is the convenience, not a gate on the capability.)

## Usage & revenue metering

A **metering dashboard** over your gateway's invocation and payment activity —
usage and revenue by agent, tenant, and time — with **usage-based billing** on
top of it. This is the finance-team surface: the raw invocation and payment
records are available to OSS via the API, but the aggregated dashboard and
billing live in the managed tier.

## Multi-tenant control plane

A **centralized control plane** to manage and govern many Zerker tenants
from one place — the operational convenience for running Zerker at fleet
scale, rather than administering each instance on its own.
